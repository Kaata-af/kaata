2.2.0 testing build 33

Update over the previous testing build while keeping your existing ledgers. These are checks to perform, not claims that phone testing is complete. Please test in English and Dari, including mirrored layouts, large text and reduced motion.

- Close and quickly reopen the notification dropdown. Check for flicker, stale rows and safe navigation after account or kaata changes.
- Use Sort & filter for customer name, balance, latest tally and custom groups. Create, rename and archive a group; move customers; confirm full kaata totals do not change when filtering. Clerks and viewers may filter but cannot change groups.
- Check the compact Given/Received amounts and counts beside a customer's balance, including older settled periods. Rejected, voided and opening balances should not inflate those totals. Search should not change the summary.
- As owner or manager, set each direction's credit limit to a warning or clerk restriction. Test exact amounts, zero/unlimited caps, pending shared tallies, repayments and denied entries. Cloud-connected clerks need internet for each new tally; fully local paired kaatas still work offline. Hard restrictions require sign-in, an online save and cloud enrollment for every clerk. Higher roles may override limits.
- Set personal reminders once, daily or weekly, including Thursday. Allow and deny notification permission; test native date/time pickers, app restart, background delivery, tapping a reminder, Mark done, account changes and timezone changes. Reminders belong to this phone/account and never send WhatsApp automatically.
- On a fresh install, choose language, then Gregorian, Solar Hijri or Qamari dates. Check Back, interrupted onboarding, email signup/reset, restore and invitation paths. Swiping Back from the first email form should return to sign-in choices.
- Test the black search button in view-only kaatas and tally searches by notes, numbers and dates across current and settled history. Verify accepted/rejected status dots, live acceptance/rejection feedback and the neutral app colors.
- Check member names and email addresses, including a name change on another phone. Test WhatsApp sharing beside the three-dot menu and reminder loading: one share action, spinner without a page-top loading message, repeated taps and cancellation.
- Restore linked ledgers on another phone and compare current/settled tallies, checkmarks and balances after reconnecting across separate networks. Also update an existing populated app to exercise local migrations and cloud restore of groups and limits; private reminders should stay on their original phone.

Qamari uses a calculated civil calendar and may differ from local moon sighting. Test app dates and PDF exports; shared web links require the matching server/web update. Cloud credit limits require backend migration 056 before testing them. Family-ledger permissions remain deferred. Weekly growth belongs to the admin dashboard, separately from this native build. This build is for testing only and must not be promoted to production.
