Kaata 2.1: shared accounts are easier to join and keep tidy.

- Clear a shared account at zero and start a fresh page; history stays saved.
- Joining suggests the matching contact and can switch an empty kaata's currency.
- Notifications are marked read once handled; no late alerts for reviewed tallies.
- Clearer tally rows with larger review buttons.
- Exports show each tally's status, author and reviewer.
- Deleting your account keeps shared history for the other person.
