Kaata 2.1.1 adds email sign-in alongside Google and Apple.

- Create your own Kaata account with an email address, a verification code and a password.
- Choose an optional username for sign-in, and reset a forgotten password by email.
- Google and Apple users can add an email password from Account settings.
- Password changes and resets sign out older sessions on your other devices.
- Return directly to an unfinished email code step after reopening the app.
- Clearer Dari account screens and security emails, with support for Persian digits.

Your existing kaatas and cloud backups stay available.
