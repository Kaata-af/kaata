Kaata 2.3.0 testing build 47

Update in place without uninstalling or clearing data. These are checks to perform, not claims that every phone scenario has already been tested.

- App lock: enable it manually in Settings. Check Face ID or device authentication on reopening Kaata, backgrounding it, opening sheets, and canceling an unlock. It stays off until enabled.
- Customer notes: add/edit internal instructions, read them as a clerk on another updated device, clear a note, and check offline sync/restore. Notes belong to this Kaata and stay out of bills, WhatsApp messages and the linked customer's account. Editors can update them; read-only staff can view them. An untouched note must survive an unrelated contact edit.
- Credit limits: configure independent give/receive limits for each currency, check warning and approval behavior in both directions, and verify one currency never changes another's limit.
- Settlement: check an account whose old and new backdated tallies sum to zero. Settling must retain every tally, date and currency while keeping the history in the right section.
- Cards: light glossy balances, continuous currency headers on Home, currency beside the customer balance, infinite currency swiping, and readable large amounts. Direct card interactions stop autoplay; list filtering does not. WhatsApp is in the three-dot menu when the customer has no saved number.
- Layout: check list swipes without diagonal movement, stable received/gave labels, clear filter toggle above blur, one app-wide notifications settings entry, correct empty-Kaata guidance, and English/Dari/Pashto. The custom animations preference has been removed; system accessibility preferences still apply.
- Regression: shared Notify/Both can write/Require approval modes, multiple currencies, search/date filters, Add/Edit tally, calculator, reminders, bill links and balance-only messages, PDF/CSV, sign-in, and backup/restore.

Testing only: existing Team (Expo) internal TestFlight group. No App Store review, public release or external beta distribution.
