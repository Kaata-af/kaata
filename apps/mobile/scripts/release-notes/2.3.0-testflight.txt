Kaata 2.3.0 testing build 48

Update in place without uninstalling or clearing data. These are checks to perform, not claims that every phone scenario has already been tested.

- App lock: enable it manually in Settings. Check Face ID and the explicit Use phone passcode option, including canceled or failed authentication, backgrounding, opening sheets and reopening Kaata. Check the lock-screen motion with system Reduce Motion both on and off. App lock stays off until enabled.
- Customer notes: add/edit internal instructions and check the displayed editor attribution. Read them as a clerk on another updated device, clear a note, and check offline sync/restore. Notes belong to this Kaata and stay out of bills, WhatsApp messages and the linked customer's account. Editors can update them; read-only staff can view them. An untouched note must survive an unrelated contact edit.
- Credit limits: configure independent give/receive limits for each currency, check warning and approval behavior in both directions, and verify one currency never changes another's limit. Check optional owner/admin alerts when a limit is newly reached, then lowered and reached again.
- Combined credit alerts: try multiple currency conditions in one rule (all conditions must be reached) and multiple alternative rules (any rule can trigger). Balances must never be converted or added across currencies. Existing balances establish the starting point; check new threshold crossings, customer overrides, Kaata defaults and notification privacy. Protected live server capabilities remain unverified; if support is unavailable, the app must explain it and preserve the saved setting rather than claim an alert was delivered.
- Settlement: check an account whose old and new backdated tallies sum to zero. Settling must retain every tally, date and currency while keeping the history in the right section.
- Cards: check the softer cherry/rose balance colors, continuous currency headers on Home, currency beside the customer balance, infinite currency swiping, and readable large amounts. Direct card interactions stop autoplay; list filtering does not. WhatsApp is in the three-dot menu when the customer has no saved number.
- Layout: check the customer page without its removed filter popup, normal customer search/date filtering, list swipes without diagonal movement, stable received/gave labels, one app-wide notifications settings entry, correct empty-Kaata guidance, and English/Dari/Pashto. The custom animations preference has been removed; system accessibility preferences still apply.
- Regression: shared Notify/Both can write/Require approval modes, multiple currencies, search/date filters, Add/Edit tally, calculator, reminders, bill links and balance-only messages, PDF/CSV, sign-in, and backup/restore.

Testing only: existing Team (Expo) internal TestFlight group. No App Store review, public release or external beta distribution.
