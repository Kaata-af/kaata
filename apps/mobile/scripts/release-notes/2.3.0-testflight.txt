Kaata 2.3.0 testing build 46

Update in place without uninstalling or clearing data. Use disposable records for shared-account and recovery checks. These are checks to perform, not claims of completed phone testing.

- Shared links: the choices are Notify (default), Both can write, and Require approval (handshake icon). In Notify, the creator adds tallies and the recipient can only view them and receive alerts, without approving each tally. Both can write records either person's tallies immediately. Require approval retains pending/accept/reject. Existing links and unfinished invitations must keep their original mode. Check on both updated phones with disposable records, including offline retries, recipient restrictions and unlinking.
- Multiple currencies: one invitation covers the customer's configured/recorded currencies. Check independent balances, new currencies, joining, opening amounts, history and export. Do not combine different currencies into one amount.
- Cards and gestures: cards loop through currencies in both directions. List swipes filter collect/pay (received/gave on the customer page); a slow swipe or blank-space swipe should work, and repeating the same side restores All. Card taps apply that currency and direction; card swipes only change currency. Automatic motion continues after list/popup filtering and stops after direct card interaction, including card filter buttons.
- Settled history: Home hides fully settled accounts behind See settled accounts; expanding places those accounts above the Hide button. Customer pages hide prior settled tallies within the original timeline; expanding reveals full dated history. Search and reminder links can reveal old tallies. New activity, pending records or an open balance in any currency must stay visible. Folding must never change totals or records.
- Filters and layout: sort-only views hide Clear all filters. Real direction/currency/date filters keep it available. Date tags sit beside the other active tags, and matching-tally counts sit opposite Clear. No All time caption appears in customer cards. Check the centered customer name, hidden phone, card text while expanding, filter content height and RTL wrapping.
- Regression: check Amount/Note keyboard handoff, Date beneath Show more, calculator edge cases, saved-currency edits, search above the Android keyboard, English/Dari/Pashto, bills and balance-only reminders, PDF/CSV, sign-in, backup/restore, notification cold-start navigation, disabled roles and canceling an unused invitation without changing original tallies. Update in place; keep existing app data.
- Ratings still require at least a week of active use. Apple's rating card is disabled in TestFlight; its absence here is expected. Code emails retain their selectable code fields.

Known testing issue: Android notification-only alerts can retain an amber pending-clock icon even though the tally is recorded. Notification text, balances and approval-action guards remain correct.

Testing only: existing Team (Expo) internal TestFlight group. No App Store review, public release or external beta distribution.
