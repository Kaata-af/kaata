# User-feedback checklist audit

Source audit completed **October 12, 2026**, against feature commit `242bb42` on
`main`. The checklist is the user's pasted feedback, plus the viewer home-search
request from the conversation. This document records source coverage; deployment,
signed-build delivery and testing-store status belong in
[2.2.0-testing-distribution.md](2.2.0-testing-distribution.md).

**Implemented** means a reachable control and its required source behavior are
present. It does not mean phone or visual QA has passed. **Partial** identifies
unfinished deliverables. The household ledger is the only feature deliberately
**deferred by the user**. No silently omitted app feature was found.

The five additional safety/customization requests are audited below against
feature commit `d7e337b`. They followed TestFlight 33 / Play internal 53 and are
now delivered in **TestFlight 34 / Play internal 54**, from release source
`bcd3140`. Matching backend/web support is live; store production reviews and
closed testing remain unchanged. See the distribution log for independent
delivery evidence.

## Validation and remaining checks

The follow-up home/timed-range requests were built after the 34/54 delivery and
are now available in **TestFlight 35 / Play internal 55**, from feature `c549e04`
and release source `f91b9a8`:

- Home exposes Sort & filter on the left, customer search beside it and the
  black add-only plus on the right. Search includes zero-tally customers and
  combines with existing groups/sorts; viewers keep search/sort without add.
- Custom tally ranges include native start/end time controls, defaulting to
  00:00–23:59. The final minute is inclusive; saved clocks reopen unchanged,
  invalid times cannot apply, and the range chip includes dates and clocks.
  Presets still cover whole days, and whole balances/statements remain whole.

Focused development checks and native scenarios for this new batch are recorded
in [next-release-refinements.md](next-release-refinements.md). Signed builds and
testing-store delivery are verified in the distribution log; native phone checks
remain pending. Production reviews and closed testing were unchanged.

The earlier customer-tools batch passed 45 mobile suites plus TypeScript. The
safety/customization batch passed **50 mobile suites plus TypeScript: 51 checks**,
all 14 backend application packages with tests and final recovery/sync reruns,
85 web tests/build and both production-profile Hermes exports. Signed builds,
backend rollout and independent testing-store delivery are recorded in
[the distribution log](2.2.0-testing-distribution.md). Native phone testing remains
separate and pending.

Matching backend support is required for member-profile migration **055**, credit
policy/admission migration **056**, group sync/restore and Qamari shared-link
rendering. Store uploads alone do not deploy those server changes. Cloud-connected
clerks now require authenticated internet admission for every new tally, even
with warning-only/no cap; conservatively local paired notebooks retain offline
writing. Hard restrictions require cloud enrollment and an online save. See the
[credit rollout contract](customer-credit-limits.md).

Phone, actual native notification/gesture, English/Dari large-text and two-device
network/restore checks remain pending, together with marketing visual/print/export
review. The [consolidated checklist](next-release-refinements.md#consolidated-checks-after-feature-additions)
contains the scenarios. The household feature remains expressly postponed; brand
artwork/export completion is partial, not an additional deferred app feature.

The signed store profiles retain the existing solo-mode settings, but all the
requested tools and the Members → Send invite → Clerk cloud flow remain
reachable. Nearby/Bluetooth/Wi-Fi mesh is independently parked in the current
app. These testing binaries can exercise cloud owner/clerk/viewer flows, but
fresh offline QR pairing and live mesh propagation require a development build
with `MESH_PARKED` disabled and the pairing entry points restored. Changing
solo-mode configuration alone is insufficient; this distribution cannot complete
those checks through its normal interface.
